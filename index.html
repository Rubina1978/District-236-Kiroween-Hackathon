WELCOME TO THE MEADOW.

The world is enormous when you're only a few millimetres tall. Every blade of grass is cover. Every shadow could mean death. And the spiders? They're not interested in negotiations.

Inspired by Kota 236, take control of an ant soldier on a mission through a dangerous meadow. Fight your way through hostile wildlife, survive the battlefield and track down the ultimate prize.

OBJECTIVE: FIND THE WHISKEY. SURVIVE IF POSSIBLE.

Why did we make this game

Made by Dylan and Hana for the 2026 Kiroween Hackathon funded by AWS / Amazon.

The game was a response to the 'Resurrection' category in the competition, and Hana provided the brief for the game. It is a very unique submission considering that little is known about Kota 236. We used Pygame to get ourselves running, using Kiro IDE and Kiro AI.

Currently, the game features a satire company like that of amazon, called 'pennine', and that's why we are looting boxes in the game for resources. However, Dylan and Hana plan to take this game further and we will be replacing certain satire with something better for the game. Namely, instead of boxes you can loot spider eggs / dens or other ant soldier corpses.

However, whilst the game is still in the stage of submission for the Kiroween Hackathon, we have left lootable boxes in the game that provide rewards, their icons, and the 'pennine' company logo.
