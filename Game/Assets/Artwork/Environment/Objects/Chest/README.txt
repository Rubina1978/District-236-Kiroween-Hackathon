Pixel Worlds - 50 Dungeon & Dragon Castle Props (Free)
======================================================

50 isometric pixel-art props (2:1 view, light from the top left), free to use.
Each prop is a separate transparent PNG in three sizes: _1x (native), _2x and _4x
(exact nearest-neighbour upscales, no blur). Folders group the props by theme.
previews/ shows every prop at 2x.

Made with: Krea 2 Turbo by Krea AI (AI-generated), then cut, sized to the grid and turned into pixel art by SdavGha.

Tile grid and scale
-------------------
Isometric diamond tile: 64x32 px at 1x, 128x64 at 2x, 256x128 at 4x.
Real-world scale: 1 tile = 1 x 1 m, and 1 m of height is about 36 px at 1x
(a 1.7 m person is about 61 px tall). A barrel is smaller than a tile, a market stall
covers 2x2 tiles - every prop in the pack is drawn to the same scale.

Pivot: the bottom-centre of every image. Put it on the BOTTOM corner of a tile
(the corner nearest the viewer). Images have empty space added where needed so this
one rule works for every prop.

Footprint codes in the list below:
  AxB     the prop covers A tiles along the tile's lower-left edge and B tiles along its
          lower-right edge, starting from the pivot tile. 2x2 = a square of four tiles.
  small   smaller than one tile; with the usual pivot it stands in the middle of the tile.
