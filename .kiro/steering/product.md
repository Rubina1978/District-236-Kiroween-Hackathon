# District 236: Product Overview

District 236 is a spooky 2.5D forest adventure built for the Kiroween hackathon. The atmosphere is inspired by the original Point256 (Kota256) - promotional game of Czech liquer company Jan Becher from 2001 making a traditional Czech liquer Becherovka. Kota 236 won second place in the 2001 edition of the competition.

## Core idea
- The player controls an **ant** moving through a dark forest.
- **Spiders** are the enemies.
- The ant can **shoot** spiders. Ammunition is **limited**.
- **Ammo boxes** restore ammunition. **Health kits** restore health.
- A **ghost** character may give the player hints.
- The goal is to survive and reach the **final destination**, where the reward is an **Ant Whiskey bottle**.

## "2.5D" means
Flat 2D Pygame sprites with a sense of depth (for example layered backgrounds, parallax scrolling, shadows, or size changes). It does NOT mean a 3D engine. Do not introduce 3D libraries.

## Scope rules
- This is a hackathon project. Prefer a small, finished, playable game over a large unfinished one.
- Build in this rough order: window, player movement, shooting, ammo, enemies, health, items, ghost hints, final goal, polish.
- Do not add features that are not requested.
